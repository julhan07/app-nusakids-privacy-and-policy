Privacy Policy

NusaKids E-Comic

Last Updated: 1 August 2026

NusaKids E-Comic is a free educational application designed to improve elementary school students' reading interest and creative thinking through interactive digital comics based on the Profil Pelajar Pancasila.

1. Introduction

Thank you for using NusaKids E-Comic. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles user information.

2. No Personal Data Collection

NusaKids E-Comic does not collect, store, or process any personal information.

Specifically, the application does not collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Location data
  • Photos or media files
  • Contacts
  • Payment information
  • Any other personally identifiable information

3. No User Account Required

NusaKids E-Comic is completely free to use. Users can simply download the application and start reading immediately.

  • No registration required.
  • No login required.
  • No account creation required.

4. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for elementary school students. Because no personal information is collected, children can use the application without providing personal data. Parents and teachers are encouraged to accompany children while using digital learning applications.

5. Offline Learning

Most learning content is designed to be accessed directly within the application. The application does not require users to submit personal information to enjoy its educational content.

6. Third-Party Services

NusaKids E-Comic does not require users to sign in using third-party services. The application does not intentionally collect personal information through external service providers.

7. Educational Purpose

NusaKids E-Comic is developed solely for educational purposes. Its mission is to foster literacy, creativity, and character development based on the six dimensions of the Profil Pelajar Pancasila.

  • Faith in God Almighty and Noble Character
  • Global Diversity
  • Mutual Cooperation
  • Independence
  • Critical Thinking
  • Creativity

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date on this page.

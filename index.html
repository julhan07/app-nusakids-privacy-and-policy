NusaKids E-Comic

Last Updated : 1 August 2026

NusaKids E-Comic is an educational application designed to improve elementary school students' reading interest and creative thinking through interactive digital comics based on the Profil Pelajar Pancasila.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how NusaKids E-Comic collects, uses, stores, and protects user information. By using this application, users agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, we may collect:

  • User name or nickname.
  • Email address (if registration is required).
  • Learning progress.
  • Reading history.
  • Quiz or activity results.
  • Technical information such as device model, operating system, and application version.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide educational services.
  • Save learning progress.
  • Improve reading recommendations.
  • Improve application performance.
  • Fix technical issues.
  • Develop new educational features.

4. Children's Privacy

NusaKids E-Comic is intended for elementary school students. We are committed to protecting children's privacy. We do not knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children. Parents, guardians, or schools are encouraged to supervise children's use of the application.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect user information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

6. Third-Party Services

The application may use trusted third-party services such as:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics

These services may collect limited technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

7. Educational Content

All comics, illustrations, stories, and learning materials are developed to support literacy, creativity, and character development based on the six dimensions of the Profil Pelajar Pancasila:

  • Faith in God Almighty and Noble Character
  • Global Diversity
  • Mutual Cooperation
  • Independence
  • Critical Thinking
  • Creativity

8. Data Retention

User information is retained only as long as necessary to provide educational services or comply with applicable legal obligations.

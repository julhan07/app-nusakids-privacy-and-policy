Privacy Policy

NusaKids E-Comic

Last Updated: August 1, 2026

NusaKids E-Comic is a free educational application that helps elementary school students improve their reading interest, literacy skills, and creative thinking through interactive digital comics inspired by the values of the Profil Pelajar Pancasila.

1. Introduction

Welcome to NusaKids E-Comic. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles user information.

NusaKids E-Comic is designed as a simple educational application that can be used immediately after installation without requiring registration or login.

2. No Personal Data Collection

NusaKids E-Comic does not collect, store, process, or share any personal data.

The application does not request or collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Location information
  • Photos or media files
  • Contacts
  • Payment information
  • Identity documents
  • Biometric information
  • Browsing history
  • Any personally identifiable information

3. No Registration or Login

NusaKids E-Comic is completely free.

Users can simply:

  • Download the application.
  • Open the application.
  • Start reading educational comics immediately.

No account creation, sign up, registration, or login is required.

4. Children's Privacy

NusaKids E-Comic is intended for elementary school students. Since the application does not collect personal information, children can safely enjoy the educational content without submitting personal data.

Parents, guardians, and teachers are encouraged to accompany children while using digital learning applications.

5. Educational Purpose

The application is developed solely for educational purposes to encourage reading habits, creativity, and character development.

Learning materials are inspired by the six dimensions of the Profil Pelajar Pancasila:

  • Faith in God Almighty and Noble Character
  • Global Diversity
  • Mutual Cooperation
  • Independence
  • Critical Thinking
  • Creativity

6. Third-Party Services

NusaKids E-Comic does not require users to sign in with Google, Facebook, Apple, or any other third-party account.

The application is designed to operate without requesting personal information from users.

7. Internet Connection

Some application features or future content updates may require an internet connection. However, the application does not collect or transmit users' personal information.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page together with the revised "Last Updated" date.
